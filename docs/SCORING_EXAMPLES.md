# Scoring Examples

These examples are intentionally generic. Replace the domains and evidence with your own search criteria.

## Generic Requirements Do Not Prove Fit

Role:
- Senior Product Manager, Billing Platform
- Required: 7+ years PM, roadmap ownership, cross-functional leadership, communication
- Preferred: payments, pricing systems, ERP, accounting integrations

Candidate:
- 10+ years PM
- Strong technical-platform background
- No payments, billing, accounting, or ERP experience

Interpretation:
- Required qualifications are mostly generic, so Section A may look strong.
- Preferred qualifications define the real role domain.
- If the candidate has no credible bridge to billing/payments, reduce or cap the score.

Outcome:
- Surface only if the broader search intentionally includes that domain.
- Otherwise Park or Skip despite the generic PM match.

## Preferred Qualifications Can Be The Real Hiring Profile

Role:
- Product Manager, Technical Platform
- Required: 5+ years PM, technical products, cross-functional execution
- Preferred: distributed systems, platform reliability, developer workflows, cloud infrastructure

Candidate:
- Technical PM with cloud/platform exposure
- Some relevant systems adjacency
- Little or no direct ownership of the specialized platform surface

Interpretation:
- Required qualifications show baseline eligibility.
- Preferred qualifications reveal the actual screening profile.
- At highly competitive companies, missing most domain-defining preferred qualifications should cap the score.

Outcome:
- Review if the candidate has a coherent adjacent story.
- Do not rank it above roles where the candidate directly matches the infrastructure domain.

## Bridge Role Versus Random Mismatch

Bridge role:
- Product Manager, Workflow Automation
- Requires technical PM judgment, customer discovery, workflow design, data-driven iteration
- Specialized technical experience is helpful but not framed as a core requirement

Why it may pass:
- A candidate with technical-platform PM experience and relevant workflow projects may have a credible narrative.
- Score should be capped below direct-fit roles, but still surfaced.

Random mismatch:
- Product Manager, Healthcare Claims Automation
- Requires healthcare claims domain depth, payer/provider workflows, compliance, and reimbursement expertise

Why it should not pass:
- Generic AI or automation language does not overcome a domain mismatch when the role's core value depends on specialized industry knowledge.

## Title And Level Matter

Role:
- Product Manager II at a large company with mature leveling

Candidate:
- Established senior IC PM

Interpretation:
- Even if the domain fits, the level may represent a meaningful downshift.
- Adjust for a material level/scope mismatch unless the role has unusually strong fit.

Role:
- Product Manager at a 100-person startup

Candidate:
- Established senior IC PM

Interpretation:
- Startup titles are often compressed.
- Do not over-penalize the title if ownership, compensation, and scope are senior.

## Empty Or Incomplete JD

Role:
- Product Manager, Platform
- JD is missing, truncated, or mostly boilerplate

Interpretation:
- Do not infer strong fit from company/title alone.
- Cap the score until the full job description is reviewed.

Outcome:
- Review manually if the company/title looks promising.
- Do not let the bot rank it as a top lead without full evidence.
